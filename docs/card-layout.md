# Card layout

How the cards are arranged on a shared deck (`/d/…`), both in the deck and in the plan's column beside it. The builder is `PlanBuilder` in `src/components/PlanBuilder.tsx`. Cards on a saved plan (`/p/…`) tilt on hover the same way.

## Tilting on hover

Cards sit straight in their rows. While the mouse is over a card, it grows a little and tips to one side with a small spring, and it springs back when the mouse leaves. Picking a card up off the table feels loose, while the deck stays tidy.

- **Small.** A hovered card leans between 0.2° and 1° (`minRotate`/`maxRotate` in `src/components/tilt.ts`) and grows to 1.04× its size (`hoverScale`).
- **Away from where the mouse came in.** The card tips as if the mouse had nudged it on the way in, turning about its centre (`entryLean`). Coming in from the left near the top tips the top to the right, and coming in near the bottom tips the bottom away instead. Coming down onto the top edge tips that end of it down. The nearer a corner, the further it leans; coming straight at the middle of an edge lifts the card without leaning it. The push is the way the mouse was moving as it came onto the card, or straight in from the nearest edge when the browser doesn't say.
- **Mouse only.** Touch screens don't tilt, so a tap never leaves a card leaning. The tilt runs from the cards' pointer enter and leave events, and skips touch pointers. It doesn't use Motion's `onHoverStart`, which runs a frame after the event, when the event no longer says which card it was on.
- **Settled by the animations.** A card is usually still leaning and grown when it's clicked. The card flying into the plan straightens and shrinks back on the way. Opening a card's notes straightens it as it lifts and flips ([docs/card-notes.md](card-notes.md) § "Opening a card's notes").

The lean is a Motion `rotate` value, so layout animations measure cards as if they were straight. Animations that start from a card's place on screen use `untiltedBox`, which works out where the card would be if it were straight. They start from the lean the card has at that moment, read with `leanOf` from its computed transform. That lean is 0 when the card was opened from the keyboard, and part-way when the card was still springing back. `untiltedBox` also measures the card at the size it's drawn, so the animations start from a grown card's real size.

The plan's column has extra padding so a hovered card's edges and corners aren't clipped where it scrolls.

## The plan column

On `/d/…`, and when editing a plan, the deck takes up most of the width of the page, and the plan is a thin column on its right. The deck comes first in the page too, so the keyboard and screen readers reach it before the plan. The page's title, with anything under it (like **Editing a plan**) centred below it, and the bar with **Save plan** (see [Save plan and Clear plan](#save-plan-and-clear-plan)), go across the top of both.

- **Running down.** Each row of the plan (its first row, and each group) runs down the column, one card under the next, so the plan reads from top to bottom. A group's title and notes sit above its cards, centred over them, with **Remove group** centred under them ([plans.md](plans.md) § "Groups").
- **Thin.** The column is between 200px and 270px wide on a wide screen, and its cards are never wider than 250px.
- **Stays in the window.** The column sticks to the top of the window as the deck scrolls past it. When the plan is taller than the window, the column scrolls on its own.
- **The title.** **The Plan** is at the top of the column, above the plan, and stays there while the plan scrolls under it. The cards are in the middle under it: room for a scrollbar is kept on both sides of the plan (`scrollbar-gutter: stable both-edges`), not just the right, so a scrollbar doesn't push them off centre. On a narrow screen it shrinks with the rest of the plan.
- **Fading out.** When the plan is taller than the room under the title, whichever end of it is scrolled out of sight fades out, rather than being cut off: the bottom until it's scrolled to the end, the top once it's scrolled down. The fades ease in and out as it scrolls. `PlanBuilder` marks the ends (`data-more-above`, `data-more-below` on `.plan-scroll`), and a mask does the fading. It watches the plan again whenever the plan is laid out afresh for kept picks, which replaces the element that scrolls.
- **Scrolling over it.** With the pointer over the plan, the wheel or trackpad scrolls the page when the plan fits. When it doesn't, it scrolls the plan to its end, and the next scroll goes on to the page, as the browser does for anything that scrolls within a page. It's always the window's height, even when the plan is shorter, so cards and links sliding up after a card leaves never overflow it and bring up a scrollbar for a moment. Where scrollbars take up room (with a mouse, or "always show scroll bars"), room for one is always kept beside the plan (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), so the cards are the same width whether it scrolls or not. Otherwise a picked card that started the plan scrolling would narrow the cards and re-wrap their text, and since narrower cards are shorter, the scrollbar could go away again.
- **The slot.** Until anything is picked, anywhere in the plan, the first row has a blank, card-sized dashed slot, with no words in it. The first card picked flies onto it and covers it: the slot stays where it is, under the card's place, until the card lands, and then it's gone. With reduced motion, where nothing flies, it goes straight away.
- **Draw random card.** Under the first row's cards (or the slot), a little further from them than they are from each other, is a **Draw random card** link, which picks a card at random from the ideas the filters show. It's gone when every one of them is in the plan.
- **Sliding out of the way.** When a card comes into the plan, **Draw random card**, the groups below and **Add group** slide down to make room for it, rather than jumping there. Only their place animates, never their size, so a group's fields don't stretch while it grows. They slide in a shrunk plan too ([Narrow screens](#narrow-screens)).
- **Empty groups.** An empty group's **Drag ideas here** is a short box rather than card-sized.
- **Flying in.** A card added from the deck flies across into its place at the bottom of the plan's first row (see [Flying cards](#flying-cards)). If that place is below what the column is showing, the column scrolls to it first.

The layout is `.builder` in `src/styles.css`, with `.builder-bar`, `.plan-column` and `.deck-column`.

## The grid of cards

The deck's cards, the cards in each row of the plan on a narrow screen, and each row on the plan page ([The plan page's grids](#the-plan-pages-grids)) are laid out the same way (the shared `.card-grid, .plan-column .plan-track` rule in `src/styles.css`).

- **As many columns as fit.** Each card is between 200px and 250px wide, and the columns share the width. Once they'd be wider than 250px, the grid stops getting wider and sits in the middle, with the spare room outside it.
- **A short last line is in the middle.** When the last line isn't full, its cards are centred under the ones above, not lined up on the left. So is a line with a single card, or the plan's blank slot on its own.

It's lines of cards that wrap (flexbox), not a CSS grid, since a grid can't centre a line that isn't full. Each card is one column's width, from `--columns`: how many 200px columns fit the grid's section. `--columns` is registered as a whole number (`@property`), so the page's code can read it (`columnCount` in `src/components/gridShuffle.ts`), and set it to hold a grid's columns while a column zooms. On a wide screen the plan's rows run down the column instead.

## Save plan and Clear plan

**Save plan** and **Clear plan** (or **Update Plan**, **Cancel** and **Delete plan**, when editing a plan) are in a bar above both columns, not in either of them.

- **How to start.** Until there's a plan to act on, the bar says **Pick a card from the deck** in place of the buttons. The buttons come in once a card is picked, or a group is added. One fades out as the other fades in, in the same place, and whichever is fading out can't be pressed. While editing a plan they're always there, so **Cancel** is too ([plans.md](plans.md) § "Editing a plan"). The bar keeps its height either way, so the page doesn't move.
- **Scrolls with the page.** The bar isn't sticky: it scrolls away with the page's title.
- **No background.** The bar has no background of its own. Its words (the prompt, **Clear plan** and the rest, and any error) have the dark halo that text sitting on the page's background has, so they stay readable over the galaxy and over cards scrolling under them. **Save plan** has its own background, so no halo.
- **On a narrow screen** it goes across both columns too, whichever is in use, so the buttons are never shrunk.

## Narrow screens

Below 900px wide, the two columns are still side by side, but only one of them is in use at a time.

- **One in use.** The column in use takes up five sixths of the width. The other is a strip a fifth as wide as it (about 64px on a phone, more on a wider screen), and everything in it is shrunk to a fifth of its size.
- **The whole column shrinks.** A shrunk column is laid out just as wide as when it's in use, then drawn at a fifth of the size, so it's a small picture of the plan or the deck as it would be: the same number of columns of cards, in the same places. Nothing in it moves round as it shrinks or grows. The plan's column keeps no room for a scrollbar while it's shrunk, or while it's growing or shrinking, since that room isn't shrunk with the rest; it's made up with padding, the same width as the room it keeps in use (`--scrollbar-gutter`, measured by `PlanBuilder`, and `.builder[data-switching]` while it grows or shrinks).
- **The last column used.** On a wide screen, where both columns are in use, the page keeps track of which one was used last: anything in it pressed (a card, a button, a field, even its empty space) or focused from the keyboard. When the window is made narrow enough for only one column, that one is in use and the other shrinks. If neither has been used yet, it's the deck, which is also how the page opens on a narrow screen.
- **Click to switch.** A click anywhere on the shrunk column puts it in use and shrinks the other one. The click doesn't do what's under it: a click on a card in the shrunk plan doesn't discard it. Nothing in a shrunk column can be clicked or reached from the keyboard. For keyboard and screen reader users, a shrunk column is a single button, **Show your plan** or **Show the date ideas**.
- **Animated.** The switch takes half a second: the shrunk column grows to its full size and width as the other one shrinks to a fifth. With reduced motion it's instant.
- **The plan in a grid.** The plan's rows are laid out in the same grid as the deck: as many columns as fit, each card between 200px and 250px wide. While the plan is in use there's often room for several; with only room for one, it's a single column like the wide screen's. Shrunk, it has the same columns as in use. **Draw random card**, and an empty group's **Drag ideas here**, go across the whole row.
- **As tall as the window.** Each column is at least the window's height (the plan's column is exactly that, and scrolls), so a click on the space under a shrunk column's cards switches too.
- **Cards still fly.** A card added from the deck flies into its place in the shrunk plan, shrinking as it goes, and a card discarded from the plan flies back into its place in the shrunk deck. **Draw random card** in the plan picks from the shrunk deck, and the card grows as it flies out of it.
- **Back to the same place.** As the deck shrinks, the part of it at the top of the window stays there, as far as the page, which gets much shorter, allows. The page doesn't scroll up under it, which would send its cards sliding down from the top. As it grows back, it goes back to where it was in the deck, the same way.

A shrunk column is CSS `zoom: 0.2`, which makes it take up less room on the page, unlike a transform. The switch is CSS transitions on `zoom` and on the grid's columns, which are lengths (`calc(100% - …)`) rather than `fr` so they can animate. The screen width is the media query in `src/styles.css` and `narrowScreen` in `PlanBuilder.tsx`. Which column is in use (or was used last, on a wide screen) is a `data-active` attribute on `.builder`, set from `onPointerDownCapture` and `onFocusCapture` on each column while the screen is wide, so the server's HTML is already laid out right. Only the switch buttons, and making the shrunk column inert, wait for the page's script.

Safari multiplies container units (`cqw`) inside a zoomed element by the zoom a second time, so in a shrunk column the cards' text, which is sized in the card's `cqw`, fell to its smallest size, out of proportion with the card, and the grid, whose columns are worked out in the section's `cqw`, had too few columns. `markZoomedContainerUnits` in `src/components/zoomedContainerUnits.ts` measures whether the browser does this, rather than going by its name, and marks the page with `data-zoomed-container-units`. There, each shrunk section sets `--unzoom` to one over its zoom, and every `cqw` in the cards and the grid is multiplied by it (`var(--unzoom, 1)`, so 1 everywhere else). The zoom's size goes along with it in `--shrink`, a number transitioned on the same curve as `zoom`, so it's right all through a switch too. A card's border stays a pixel on the screen however far it's shrunk, which is five in a shrunk column's own units, so its text is a few percent smaller there in every browser.

Motion works out an animation in the screen's pixels but draws it inside the zoomed column, where each pixel is a fifth of the size, so it would start only a fifth of the way back and look like a jump. So everything that slides in a shrunk column (its cards, **Draw random card**, groups, **Add group**) has its slide scaled back up to match (`unshrinkSlide`, a Motion `transformTemplate`, with `shrunkZoom` matching the zoom in `styles.css`). A card that grows or shrinks at the same time needs nothing more, since a change of size is a ratio, the same in either units. So with the deck in use, a second card picked makes the first slide over in the shrunk plan to make room. Cards keep the same `layout` and no shared `layoutId` whichever column is in use, as changing what Motion registers about a card after it has appeared leaves it confused; only the slide correction comes and goes, and Motion reads that afresh each frame. A switch doesn't upset the columns that stay mounted: Motion measures as React renders, before the transitions have moved anything, so it has nothing to animate, and the transitions then run without React. Keeping the deck's place scrolls the page each frame of the switch (`switchDuration`) to the top of the deck plus how far down it the window was, times the deck's zoom at that moment (`deckAnchor`). The flying card works out a slot's place from its offsets, and scales them by how much the column is shrunk on screen.

## Shuffling to a new number of columns

When a grid of cards gains or loses a column, its cards shuffle into their new places rather than jumping there. That's the deck's grid on any screen, the plan's grid on a narrow one, and the plan page's grids, as the window is resized or a column grows or shrinks on a switch. Each card slides from where it was to where it now goes, over 450ms, and a card part way through a slide carries on from where it is.

- **Only on screen.** A card slides when it's on screen both before and after. One that arrives on screen from off it fades in where it lands instead: sliding, it would come flying in from past the edge of the window. One that ends up off screen just goes there.
- **While a column grows or shrinks.** A switch on a narrow screen doesn't change any grid's columns ([Narrow screens](#narrow-screens) - the whole column shrinks). Crossing 900px does, as the columns go from side by side to one shrunk. Then each grid keeps the columns it had while the column zooms, and shuffles to its new columns once the zoom is over. Shuffling part way through would send cards a long way across a column that's itself moving and changing size.

This is `useGridShuffle` in `src/components/gridShuffle.ts`, not Motion: Motion only animates on a React render, and a grid changes shape as the window does, before React knows. It watches each grid's size, remembers where every card was (after every render too, so picks and discards count), and when the grid's column count changes, animates each card's CSS `translate` from its old place to its new one. `translate` is apart from the `transform` Motion animates, so a hovered card's tilt and a shuffle add up. Places are offsets within the column's section, which is positioned, so they're in the section's own units even while it's shrunk, and a grid re-centring as it narrows counts. The columns are held with inline styles (`hold`), and let go on a timer, not a React render, so Motion doesn't see the grid change and animate it as well. With reduced motion there's no shuffle.

## Flying cards

A card picked from the deck flies into its place in the plan, and a card discarded from the plan flies back into its place in the deck, on any screen size. The flight is a stand-in drawn above the page (`FlyingCard` in `src/components/FlyingCard.tsx`), starting as the card was, leaning and grown if it was hovered, and straightening as it goes. The real card waits hidden in its new place until the stand-in lands on it. With reduced motion there's no flight, and the card is just in its new place.

- **Clear plan.** Every card in the plan, groups included, flies back into the deck at once, from wherever it is: the plan's column on a wide screen, or either column in use or shrunk on a narrow one.
- **Nothing left behind.** The card leaving the deck fades out under the stand-in, and is gone once it has. A card in the plan goes straight away.
- **Anywhere.** The stand-in lands on the card's new place even when that's in a shrunk column, a scrolled plan, or off the screen. A discarded card that the deck's filters hide has nowhere to land, so it doesn't fly.

- **Its own size.** The stand-in is laid out at the card's own size, and scaled to land on its slot. In a shrunk column that's a fifth of the card's size. Laid out that small, its text couldn't shrink to match, as the card's font sizes have a least size, so it spilled out of the card and then jumped as the real card took its place. The shrink is read from the slot's `currentCSSZoom`.

The stand-in is used both ways, rather than Motion's shared layout (`layoutId`), which lost track of cards as they moved between a plan and a deck that could each be shrunk. It measures its slot from offsets, as the slot may have a layout-animation transform on it. The deck's grid is positioned, so it's the slot's offset parent inside the shrunk deck, and it holds cards that are fading out of the deck in place (`popLayout`).

Cards leaving the deck only fade, and don't shrink. The hover tilt and spring back (`tiltCard`, `leaveCard`) animate the same card's `scale`, and a mouse leaving a card as it went would take over the shrink, which then never finished. The card was left in the deck, invisible but still clickable.

## The plan page's grids

On the plan page (`/p/…`), the plan's first row, and each group's, is a grid of cards, the same as the deck's ([The grid of cards](#the-grid-of-cards)): as many columns as fit the page, wrapping onto more lines, rather than a row that scrolls sideways. A group's title and notes are above its grid, on any screen ([plans.md](plans.md) § "Groups").

The grids shuffle to a new number of columns as the window is resized, as the deck's do ([Shuffling to a new number of columns](#shuffling-to-a-new-number-of-columns)). Nothing zooms there, so nothing is held. This is `PlanView` in `src/components/PlanView.tsx`, whose rows are `.card-grid`s.

## Reordering the plan

The cards in the plan's column can be put in a different order, and moved between the plan's first row and its groups ([plans.md](plans.md) § "Groups"). The plan starts in the order the cards were picked (see [deck-sorting.md](deck-sorting.md) § "How it fits with filters and the plan"), and the new order is the one that's shared when **Save plan** is pressed.

- **By dragging it.** With a mouse, a card in the plan can be dragged from anywhere on it, up or down its row or onto another one, and the other cards slide out of its way. The card itself stays where it would land, faded, while it follows the pointer. The card following the pointer keeps showing its options, grip and edit button, as they were when it was picked up, though none of them can be pressed while it's dragged. The faded card never shows them, even with the pointer over it, and nor do the other cards in the plan while the card is dragged over them, as they're behind it. Let go with the mouse still over the card's new place, the card keeps showing its options straight from the stand-in, with no gap where they disappear, until the mouse leaves it. It lands in whichever row the pointer is over, or the nearest one. Where it goes in that row is decided by the pointer, not by the dragged card: with the pointer over any part of another card, it takes that card's place, and that card moves up or down one to make room. It then stays there until the pointer moves onto another card. With the pointer in a gap between cards, or in an empty row, it goes where the pointer has passed the middles of the cards there, going down the row. When the row is a grid of several columns ([Narrow screens](#narrow-screens)), that's in reading order: after the cards in lines above the pointer, and after those to its left in the same line. Let go, it settles into that place. A press that doesn't move is still a click, so the options on each side work as before.
- **The grip.** A card in the plan has a grip, six white dots in a half circle hanging from the middle of its top edge, in the shade behind the card's options. The options sit a little lower on these cards to clear it, and the white line between them starts just below the grip and fades out with the shade as usual (see [card-notes.md](card-notes.md) § "Card actions"). Only the grip itself takes clicks. It shows whenever the card's options do (see [card-notes.md](card-notes.md) § "When the options show"). Cards in the deck don't have one.
- **Only the grip, on a touch screen.** A touch only drags the card from its grip, so a swipe on the rest of the card still scrolls the plan. The grip shows after a tap, like the options, and can then be dragged.
- **Not a click.** Pressing the grip, or letting go of a drag over the card, doesn't discard the card or open its notes. A drag doesn't start from a link in the description, which is followed as usual, and doesn't select the page's text.
- **Leans back from the drag.** A dragged card leans against the way it's going, like a card held by its bottom edge: dragged right, its top tips left. It leans further the faster it goes, up to 5° (`dragLean`, `maxDragRotate` in `src/components/tilt.ts`), straightens as it slows or stops, and stands straight again when it's let go.
- **Scrolls with the drag.** When the plan is taller than its column, dragging a card up to the top or bottom of the column scrolls it, so a card can be moved past cards and groups that are out of sight. The column starts scrolling once the dragged card comes within 32px of its edge (or of the window's edge, if the column goes past it), and goes faster the further the card is pushed past that, up to 900px a second (`edgeScrollSpeed` in `src/components/edgeScroll.ts`). It keeps going while the card is held there. Only the end the card has been dragged towards counts, so picking up a card that's already half out of sight doesn't set the plan scrolling. While that end of the column is out of the window, as it is before the column has stuck to the top of the window, the page scrolls instead, until it's in.
- **From the keyboard.** The grip is a button (**Move …**). The up and down arrow keys (or left and right) move the card one place up or down the plan. Past the bottom of a row it goes onto the top of the row under it, and past the top onto the bottom of the row above, so it steps through each group in turn, empty ones included (`stepCard` in `src/lib/plan-picks.ts`). The grip keeps focus so it can be moved again.
- **Kept in the browser.** The kept picks follow the new order straight away, so a reload keeps it ([plans.md](plans.md) § "Picks are kept in the browser").

The cards' hover handlers are in `src/components/cardControls.tsx`, shared with the plan page. The drag is `usePlanDrag` in `src/components/PlanCard.tsx`, not Motion's `Reorder`, which only reorders within one list. Each card in the plan is a `PlanCard`, and each row a track marked with `data-plan-row`. Once a press has moved 4px, a stand-in (`DragStandIn`) is drawn above the page, following the pointer, while the card itself moves from row to row in the picks. Places are worked out from the cards' offsets down the row, which layout animations don't move, so cards sliding out of the way don't change the answer. The lean, the scrolling and the place run once a frame for as long as the drag lasts, and the place again on every move of the pointer. The plan's rows aren't in an `AnimatePresence`: a card moved to another row would otherwise linger in the one it left.
